ReviewDesk also uses published reporting guidance and methods, each credited in the files ReviewDesk writes:

  PRISMA 2020 checklist and flow diagram
    Page MJ, McKenzie JE, Bossuyt PM, et al. The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting
    systematic reviews. BMJ 2021;372:n71. doi:10.1136/bmj.n71. Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
    International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The PRISMA 2020 checklist that
    ReviewDesk writes quotes the 27 checklist items word for word and adds columns of its own; the flow diagram is
    adapted from the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram template (ReviewDesk draws it with its own layout and fills in the counts).

  PRESS 2015 (Peer Review of Electronic Search Strategies)
    McGowan J, Sampson M, Salzwedel DM, Cogo E, Foerster V, Lefebvre C. PRESS Peer Review of Electronic Search
    Strategies: 2015 guideline statement. J Clin Epidemiol 2016;75:40-6. The search worksheet's six prompts are
    ReviewDesk's own words, covering the areas of PRESS 2015; they are not the PRESS checklist, which is published
    under its own licence (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and is what a librarian uses for a formal peer review.

  GRADE
    The meaning of each level of certainty follows Balshem H, Helfand M, Schünemann HJ, et al. GRADE guidelines: 3.
    Rating the quality of evidence. J Clin Epidemiol 2011;64:401-6; for results that were not pooled, Murad MH,
    Mustafa RA, Schünemann HJ, Sultan S, Santesso N. Rating the certainty in evidence in the absence of a single
    estimate of effect. Evid Based Med 2017;22:85-7. The suggested plain-language statements follow Santesso N,
    Glenton C, Dahm P, et al. GRADE guidelines 26: informative statements to communicate the findings of systematic
    reviews of interventions. J Clin Epidemiol 2020;119:126-35.

  Risk-of-bias tools (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, ROBINS-E, QUADAS-2, PROBAST+AI, QUIPS)
    ReviewDesk uses only each tool's name, the names of its domains, its judgement words and its citation, and
    states each tool's rule for the overall judgement in its own words. It reproduces none of the tools' signalling
    questions or guidance: use the official tool, linked from ReviewDesk, for those. RoB 2, ROBINS-I and ROBINS-E
    are published by their authors under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (riskofbias.info).
